Uttar Pradesh at a glance

The 75 districts with a 2011 figure had about 20.67 crore people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Firozabad (24,98,156). The most populous, Prayagraj, has about 6.8 times as many as the least populous, Mahoba.

By area the largest is Kheri (7,680 sq km) and the smallest is Hapur (660 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Uttar Pradesh?

Uttar Pradesh has 75 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Uttar Pradesh by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Prayagraj is the most populous district of Uttar Pradesh, with 59,54,391 people.

Which is the smallest district of Uttar Pradesh by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Mahoba is the least populous, with 8,75,958 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Uttar Pradesh by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Kheri is the largest at 7,680 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
