Kannauj district, Uttar Pradesh

Kannauj is one of the 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 16.6 lakh people.

Population in context

With 16,56,616 people in 2011, Kannauj ranks 60th among the 75 districts of Uttar Pradesh for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Prayagraj (59,54,391) and the least populous is Mahoba (8,75,958).

District boundaries change. If Kannauj has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
