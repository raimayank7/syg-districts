Madurai district, Tamil Nadu

Madurai is one of the 38 districts of Tamil Nadu. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 30.4 lakh people.

Population in context

With 30,38,252 people in 2011, Madurai ranks 9th among the 37 districts of Tamil Nadu for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Chennai (46,46,732) and the least populous is Perambalur (5,65,223).

District boundaries change. If Madurai has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Madurai in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Madurai ranks 118th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Madurai has about 2.2 times that. Within Tamil Nadu, it holds about 4% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Madurai (3,497 sq km) ranks 341st of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 20th of 32 in Tamil Nadu. The largest in Tamil Nadu is Coimbatore (7,469 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 869 people for every square kilometre. That is above the figure for Tamil Nadu as a whole, about 544, worked out from the same district figures.
