Dindigul district, Tamil Nadu

Dindigul is one of the 38 districts of Tamil Nadu. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 21.6 lakh people.

Population in context

With 21,59,775 people in 2011, Dindigul ranks 17th among the 37 districts of Tamil Nadu for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Chennai (46,46,732) and the least populous is Perambalur (5,65,223).

District boundaries change. If Dindigul has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
