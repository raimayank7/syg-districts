Gujarat at a glance

The 33 districts with a 2011 figure had about 6.76 crore people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Valsad (17,05,678). The most populous, Ahmedabad, has about 32 times as many as the least populous, Dangs.

By area the largest is Kachchh (45,674 sq km) and the smallest is Dangs (1,764 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Newer districts

Districts of Gujarat formed since 2012, by the year Wikidata records: Vav-Tharad (2025), Chhotaudepur (2013), Devbhumi Dwarka (2013), Mahisagar (2013), Morbi (2013).

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Gujarat?

Gujarat has 34 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Gujarat by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Ahmedabad is the most populous district of Gujarat, with 72,14,225 people.

Which is the smallest district of Gujarat by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Dangs is the least populous, with 2,28,291 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Gujarat by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Kachchh is the largest at 45,674 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
