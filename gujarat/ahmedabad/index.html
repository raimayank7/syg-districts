Ahmedabad district, Gujarat

Ahmedabad is one of the 34 districts of Gujarat. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 72.1 lakh people.

Population in context

With 72,14,225 people in 2011, Ahmedabad ranks 1st among the 33 districts of Gujarat for which we have a 2011 figure. That makes it the most populous of them. The least populous is Dangs (2,28,291).

District boundaries change. If Ahmedabad has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Ahmedabad in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Ahmedabad ranks 7th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Ahmedabad has about 5.2 times that. Within Gujarat, it holds about 11% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Ahmedabad (8,107 sq km) ranks 84th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 8th of 28 in Gujarat. The largest in Gujarat is Kachchh (45,674 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 890 people for every square kilometre. That is well above the figure for Gujarat as a whole, about 292, worked out from the same district figures. A high figure usually means a large city lies inside the district.
