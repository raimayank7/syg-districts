Sabar Kantha district, Gujarat

Sabar Kantha is one of the 34 districts of Gujarat. Its headquarters is Himatnagar. At the 2011 census it had about 24.3 lakh people.

Population in context

With 24,28,589 people in 2011, Sabar Kantha ranks 8th among the 33 districts of Gujarat for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Ahmedabad (72,14,225) and the least populous is Dangs (2,28,291).

District boundaries change. If Sabar Kantha has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
