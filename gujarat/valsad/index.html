Valsad district, Gujarat

Valsad is one of the 34 districts of Gujarat. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 17.1 lakh people.

Population in context

With 17,05,678 people in 2011, Valsad ranks 17th among the 33 districts of Gujarat for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Ahmedabad (72,14,225) and the least populous is Dangs (2,28,291).

District boundaries change. If Valsad has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Valsad in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Valsad ranks 284th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Valsad has about 1.2 times that. Within Gujarat, it holds about 3% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Valsad (2,947 sq km) ranks 403rd of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 23rd of 28 in Gujarat. The largest in Gujarat is Kachchh (45,674 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 579 people for every square kilometre. That is above the figure for Gujarat as a whole, about 292, worked out from the same district figures.
