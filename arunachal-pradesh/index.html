Arunachal Pradesh at a glance

The 17 districts with a 2011 figure had about 13.9 lakh people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Upper Subansiri (83,448). The most populous, Papum Pare, has about 22 times as many as the least populous, Dibang Valley.

By area the largest is West Siang (8,325 sq km) and the smallest is Tawang (2,172 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Arunachal Pradesh?

Arunachal Pradesh has 27 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Arunachal Pradesh by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Papum Pare is the most populous district of Arunachal Pradesh, with 1,76,573 people.

Which is the smallest district of Arunachal Pradesh by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Dibang Valley is the least populous, with 8,004 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Arunachal Pradesh by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, West Siang is the largest at 8,325 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
