Anjaw district, Arunachal Pradesh

Anjaw is one of the 27 districts of Arunachal Pradesh. Its headquarters is Hawai. At the 2011 census it had about 21,200 people.

Population in context

With 21,167 people in 2011, Anjaw ranks 15th among the 17 districts of Arunachal Pradesh for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Papum Pare (1,76,573) and the least populous is Dibang Valley (8,004).

District boundaries change. If Anjaw has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Anjaw in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Anjaw ranks 709th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Anjaw has about 2% of that. Within Arunachal Pradesh, it holds about 2% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Anjaw (6,190 sq km) ranks 152nd of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 5th of 14 in Arunachal Pradesh. The largest in Arunachal Pradesh is West Siang (8,325 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 3 people for every square kilometre. That is well below the figure for Arunachal Pradesh as a whole, about 18, worked out from the same district figures. A low figure usually points to hills, forest, desert or other thinly settled country.
