Goa at a glance

The official directory lists 3 districts for Goa. A headquarters is on record for 2 of them and a 2011 census figure for 2. Where the table says "Not recorded", the district was formed or redrawn after the 2011 census, or the open database we use has no checked value for it; the district itself is real and is in the government directory.

Tap a name for the district's own page, which gives its official website. That website is the place for current facts such as the collector's office, sub-divisions and services.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Goa?

Goa has 3 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Goa by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, North Goa is the most populous district of Goa, with 8,18,008 people.

Which is the smallest district of Goa by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, South Goa is the least populous, with 6,40,537 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Goa by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, South Goa is the largest at 1,966 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.

About these facts. The list of districts is the official one from iGOD, the Government of India web directory, as on 11 November 2026.
