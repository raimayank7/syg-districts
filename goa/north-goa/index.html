North Goa district, Goa

North Goa is one of the 3 districts of Goa. Its headquarters is Panaji. At the 2011 census it had about 8.2 lakh people.

Population in context

With 8,18,008 people in 2011, North Goa ranks 1st among the 2 districts of Goa for which we have a 2011 figure. That makes it the most populous of them. The least populous is South Goa (6,40,537).

District boundaries change. If North Goa has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

North Goa in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, North Goa ranks 497th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so North Goa has about 59% of that. Within Goa, it holds about 56% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, North Goa (1,736 sq km) ranks 542nd of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 2nd of 2 in Goa. It is the smallest district of Goa by area.

In 2011 it had about 471 people for every square kilometre. That is above the figure for Goa as a whole, about 394, worked out from the same district figures.
