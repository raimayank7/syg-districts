Kargil district, Ladakh

Kargil is one of the 8 districts of Ladakh, a union territory. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 1.4 lakh people.

Population in context

With 1,40,802 people in 2011, Kargil ranks 1st among the 2 districts of Ladakh for which we have a 2011 figure. That makes it the most populous of them. The least populous is Leh Ladakh (1,33,487).

District boundaries change. If Kargil has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
