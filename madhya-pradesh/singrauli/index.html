Singrauli district, Madhya Pradesh

Singrauli is one of the 55 districts of Madhya Pradesh. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 11.8 lakh people.

Population in context

With 11,78,273 people in 2011, Singrauli ranks 34th among the 55 districts of Madhya Pradesh for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Indore (32,76,697) and the least populous is Pandhurna (3,74,310).

District boundaries change. If Singrauli has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
