Madhya Pradesh at a glance

The 55 districts with a 2011 figure had about 7.54 crore people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Sehore (13,11,332). The most populous, Indore, has about 8.8 times as many as the least populous, Pandhurna.

By area the largest is Chhindwara (11,815 sq km) and the smallest is Alirajpur (2,165 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Newer districts

Districts of Madhya Pradesh formed since 2012, by the year Wikidata records: Maihar (2023), Mauganj (2023), Pandhurna (2023), Niwari (2018), Agar-Malwa (2013).

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Madhya Pradesh?

Madhya Pradesh has 55 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Madhya Pradesh by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Indore is the most populous district of Madhya Pradesh, with 32,76,697 people.

Which is the smallest district of Madhya Pradesh by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Pandhurna is the least populous, with 3,74,310 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Madhya Pradesh by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Chhindwara is the largest at 11,815 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
