Akola district, Maharashtra

Akola is one of the 36 districts of Maharashtra. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 18.1 lakh people.

Population in context

With 18,13,906 people in 2011, Akola ranks 26th among the 36 districts of Maharashtra for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Thane (1,10,60,148) and the least populous is Sindhudurg (8,49,651).

District boundaries change. If Akola has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
