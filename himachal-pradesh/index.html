Districts of Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh is a state with 12 districts in the official directory. Going by 2011 census figures, the most populous is Kangra (15,10,075 people) and the least populous is Lahaul And Spiti (31,564).

Himachal Pradesh at a glance

The 12 districts with a 2011 figure had about 68.6 lakh people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Chamba (5,19,080). The most populous, Kangra, has about 48 times as many as the least populous, Lahaul And Spiti.

By area the largest is Lahaul And Spiti (13,835 sq km) and the smallest is Hamirpur (1,118 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Himachal Pradesh?

Himachal Pradesh has 12 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Himachal Pradesh by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Kangra is the most populous district of Himachal Pradesh, with 15,10,075 people.

Which is the smallest district of Himachal Pradesh by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Lahaul And Spiti is the least populous, with 31,564 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Himachal Pradesh by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Lahaul And Spiti is the largest at 13,835 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
