How to use this page

Each card is a state or union territory with the number of districts it has in the official directory. Tap one for its full list: every district with its headquarters, 2011 population and area, which you can sort and search. From there each district has a page of its own.

Big lists and small ones

The longest lists are Uttar Pradesh (75), Madhya Pradesh (55), Rajasthan (41), Bihar (38), Tamil Nadu (38). At the other end, Chandigarh and Lakshadweep have a single district each. A state's number of districts follows its own history and decisions more than its size: states create new districts by their own orders, and several have added many in recent years.

Why the count keeps changing

New districts are carved out of old ones, and now and then two are merged or one is renamed. The directory we use is the Government of India's own, read on 11 November 2026. If a district announced last month is missing, it has not reached that directory yet. Older figures, such as the 2011 census, belong to the boundaries of that time.
