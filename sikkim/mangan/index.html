Mangan district, Sikkim

Mangan is one of the 6 districts of Sikkim. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 43,700 people.

Population in context

With 43,709 people in 2011, Mangan ranks 5th among the 5 districts of Sikkim for which we have a 2011 figure. That makes it the least populous of them. The most populous is Gangtok (2,83,583).

District boundaries change. If Mangan has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Mangan in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Mangan ranks 700th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Mangan has about 3% of that. Within Sikkim, it holds about 6% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Mangan (4,226 sq km) ranks 269th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 1st of 4 in Sikkim. It is the largest district of Sikkim by area.

In 2011 it had about 10 people for every square kilometre. That is well below the figure for Sikkim as a whole, about 61, worked out from the same district figures. A low figure usually points to hills, forest, desert or other thinly settled country.
