Noklak district, Nagaland

Noklak is one of the 17 districts of Nagaland. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 59,300 people.

Population in context

With 59,300 people in 2011, Noklak ranks 13th among the 17 districts of Nagaland for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Dimapur (3,78,811) and the least populous is Meluri (22,558).

Noklak was formed in 2017, after the 2011 census. The 2011 figure is the one Wikidata records for the area of the district.

Noklak in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Noklak ranks 688th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Noklak has about 4% of that. Within Nagaland, it holds about 3% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Noklak (1,152 sq km) ranks 596th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 6th of 7 in Nagaland. The largest in Nagaland is Peren (2,300 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 51 people for every square kilometre. That is well below the figure for Nagaland as a whole, about 110, worked out from the same district figures. A low figure usually points to hills, forest, desert or other thinly settled country.
