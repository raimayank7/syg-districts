Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Telangana?

Telangana has 33 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Telangana by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Ranga Reddy is the most populous district of Telangana, with 52,96,741 people.

Which is the smallest district of Telangana by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Jayashankar Bhupalapally is the least populous, with 4,16,763 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Telangana by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Adilabad is the largest at 16,128 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
