Ranga Reddy district, Telangana

Ranga Reddy is one of the 33 districts of Telangana. Its headquarters is Hyderabad. At the 2011 census it had about 53 lakh people.

Population in context

With 52,96,741 people in 2011, Ranga Reddy ranks 1st among the 15 districts of Telangana for which we have a 2011 figure. That makes it the most populous of them. The least populous is Jayashankar Bhupalapally (4,16,763).

District boundaries change. If Ranga Reddy has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Ranga Reddy in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Ranga Reddy ranks 16th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Ranga Reddy has about 3.8 times that. Within Telangana, it holds about 14% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Ranga Reddy (5,031 sq km) ranks 209th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 7th of 29 in Telangana. The largest in Telangana is Adilabad (16,128 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 1,053 people for every square kilometre. That is well above the figure for Telangana as a whole, about 491, worked out from the same district figures. A high figure usually means a large city lies inside the district.
