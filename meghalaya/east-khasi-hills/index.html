East Khasi Hills district, Meghalaya

East Khasi Hills is one of the 12 districts of Meghalaya. Its headquarters is Shillong. At the 2011 census it had about 8.3 lakh people.

Population in context

With 8,25,922 people in 2011, East Khasi Hills ranks 1st among the 11 districts of Meghalaya for which we have a 2011 figure. That makes it the most populous of them. The least populous is South West Khasi Hills (1,10,152).

District boundaries change. If East Khasi Hills has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

East Khasi Hills in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, East Khasi Hills ranks 495th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so East Khasi Hills has about 60% of that. Within Meghalaya, it holds about 24% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, East Khasi Hills (2,752 sq km) ranks 427th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 3rd of 11 in Meghalaya. The largest in Meghalaya is West Khasi Hills (5,247 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 300 people for every square kilometre. That is well above the figure for Meghalaya as a whole, about 139, worked out from the same district figures. A high figure usually means a large city lies inside the district.
