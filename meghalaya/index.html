Districts of Meghalaya

Meghalaya is a state with 12 districts in the official directory. Going by 2011 census figures, the most populous is East Khasi Hills (8,25,922 people) and the least populous is South West Khasi Hills (1,10,152).

Meghalaya at a glance

The 11 districts with a 2011 figure had about 33.8 lakh people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Ri Bhoi (2,58,840). The most populous, East Khasi Hills, has about 7.5 times as many as the least populous, South West Khasi Hills.

By area the largest is West Khasi Hills (5,247 sq km) and the smallest is South West Garo Hills (822 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Newer districts

Districts of Meghalaya formed since 2012, by the year Wikidata records: Eastern West Khasi Hills (2021), East Jaintia Hills (2012), North Garo Hills (2012), South West Garo Hills (2012), South West Khasi Hills (2012), West Jaintia Hills (2012).

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Meghalaya?

Meghalaya has 12 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Meghalaya by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, East Khasi Hills is the most populous district of Meghalaya, with 8,25,922 people.

Which is the smallest district of Meghalaya by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, South West Khasi Hills is the least populous, with 1,10,152 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Meghalaya by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, West Khasi Hills is the largest at 5,247 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.

About these facts. The list of districts is the official one from iGOD, the Government of India web directory, as on 11 November 2026.
