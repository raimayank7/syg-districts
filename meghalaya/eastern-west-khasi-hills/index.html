Eastern West Khasi Hills district, Meghalaya

Eastern West Khasi Hills is one of the 12 districts of Meghalaya. Its headquarters is Mairang. At the 2011 census it had about 1.3 lakh people.

Population in context

With 1,31,451 people in 2011, Eastern West Khasi Hills ranks 9th among the 11 districts of Meghalaya for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is East Khasi Hills (8,25,922) and the least populous is South West Khasi Hills (1,10,152).

Eastern West Khasi Hills was formed in 2021, after the 2011 census. The 2011 figure is the one Wikidata records for the area of the district.
