East Jaintia Hills district, Meghalaya

East Jaintia Hills is one of the 12 districts of Meghalaya. Its headquarters is Khliehriat. At the 2011 census it had about 1.2 lakh people.

Population in context

With 1,22,436 people in 2011, East Jaintia Hills ranks 10th among the 11 districts of Meghalaya for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is East Khasi Hills (8,25,922) and the least populous is South West Khasi Hills (1,10,152).

East Jaintia Hills was formed in 2012, after the 2011 census. The 2011 figure is the one Wikidata records for the area of the district.

East Jaintia Hills in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, East Jaintia Hills ranks 667th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so East Jaintia Hills has about 9% of that. Within Meghalaya, it holds about 4% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, East Jaintia Hills (2,115 sq km) ranks 506th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 5th of 11 in Meghalaya. The largest in Meghalaya is West Khasi Hills (5,247 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 58 people for every square kilometre. That is well below the figure for Meghalaya as a whole, about 139, worked out from the same district figures. A low figure usually points to hills, forest, desert or other thinly settled country.
