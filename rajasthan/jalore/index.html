Jalore district, Rajasthan

Jalore is one of the 41 districts of Rajasthan. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 18.3 lakh people.

Population in context

With 18,28,730 people in 2011, Jalore ranks 17th among the 37 districts of Rajasthan for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Jaipur (66,26,178) and the least populous is Salumbar (5,70,775).

District boundaries change. If Jalore has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Jalore in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Jalore ranks 259th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Jalore has about 1.3 times that. Within Rajasthan, it holds about 3% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.
