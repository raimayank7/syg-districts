Subarnapur district, Odisha

Subarnapur is one of the 30 districts of Odisha. Its headquarters is Sonepur. At the 2011 census it had about 6.1 lakh people.

Population in context

With 6,10,183 people in 2011, Subarnapur ranks 26th among the 30 districts of Odisha for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Ganjam (35,29,031) and the least populous is Debagada (3,12,520).

District boundaries change. If Subarnapur has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Subarnapur in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Subarnapur ranks 551st by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Subarnapur has about 44% of that. Within Odisha, it holds less than 2% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Subarnapur (2,284 sq km) ranks 483rd of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 28th of 30 in Odisha. The largest in Odisha is Mayurbhanj (10,418 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 267 people for every square kilometre. That is close to the figure for Odisha as a whole, about 273, worked out from the same district figures. So it is fairly typical of the state in how closely people live.
