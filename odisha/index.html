Odisha at a glance

The 30 districts with a 2011 figure had about 4.20 crore people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Anugola (12,73,821). The most populous, Ganjam, has about 11 times as many as the least populous, Debagada.

By area the largest is Mayurbhanj (10,418 sq km) and the smallest is Jagatsinghapur (1,759 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Odisha?

Odisha has 30 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Odisha by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Ganjam is the most populous district of Odisha, with 35,29,031 people.

Which is the smallest district of Odisha by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Debagada is the least populous, with 3,12,520 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Odisha by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Mayurbhanj is the largest at 10,418 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
