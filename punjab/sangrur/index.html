Sangrur district, Punjab

Sangrur is one of the 23 districts of Punjab. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 16.6 lakh people.

Population in context

With 16,55,169 people in 2011, Sangrur ranks 7th among the 22 districts of Punjab for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Ludhiana (34,98,739) and the least populous is Barnala (5,95,527).

District boundaries change. If Sangrur has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Sangrur in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Sangrur ranks 298th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Sangrur has about 1.2 times that. Within Punjab, it holds about 6% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Sangrur (3,685 sq km) ranks 323rd of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 3rd of 20 in Punjab. The largest in Punjab is Ferozepur (5,305 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 449 people for every square kilometre. That is below the figure for Punjab as a whole, about 565, worked out from the same district figures.
