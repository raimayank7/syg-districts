Fatehgarh Sahib district, Punjab

Fatehgarh Sahib is one of the 23 districts of Punjab. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 6 lakh people.

Population in context

With 6,00,163 people in 2011, Fatehgarh Sahib ranks 21st among the 22 districts of Punjab for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Ludhiana (34,98,739) and the least populous is Barnala (5,95,527).

District boundaries change. If Fatehgarh Sahib has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
