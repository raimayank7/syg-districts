Ferozepur district, Punjab

Ferozepur is one of the 23 districts of Punjab. Its headquarters is Firozpur. At the 2011 census it had about 20.3 lakh people.

Population in context

With 20,29,074 people in 2011, Ferozepur ranks 5th among the 22 districts of Punjab for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Ludhiana (34,98,739) and the least populous is Barnala (5,95,527).

District boundaries change. If Ferozepur has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
