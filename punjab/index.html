Punjab at a glance

The 22 districts with a 2011 figure had about 2.94 crore people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Moga (9,95,746). The most populous, Ludhiana, has about 5.9 times as many as the least populous, Barnala.

By area the largest is Ferozepur (5,305 sq km) and the smallest is S.A.S Nagar (424 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Punjab?

Punjab has 23 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Punjab by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Ludhiana is the most populous district of Punjab, with 34,98,739 people.

Which is the smallest district of Punjab by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Barnala is the least populous, with 5,95,527 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Punjab by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Ferozepur is the largest at 5,305 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.

About these facts. The list of districts is the official one from iGOD, the Government of India web directory, as on 11 November 2026. Headquarters, 2011 population, area and other details are from Wikidata, an open database edited by volunteers, read on 11 November 2026. They can contain mistakes.
