Baksa district, Assam

Baksa is one of the 35 districts of Assam. Its headquarters is Mushalpur. At the 2011 census it had about 9.5 lakh people.

Population in context

With 9,50,075 people in 2011, Baksa ranks 16th among the 33 districts of Assam for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Dhubri (19,49,258) and the least populous is Majuli (1,67,304).

District boundaries change. If Baksa has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
