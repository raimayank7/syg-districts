Chirang district, Assam

Chirang is one of the 35 districts of Assam. Its headquarters is Kajalgaon. At the 2011 census it had about 4.8 lakh people.

Population in context

With 4,82,162 people in 2011, Chirang ranks 27th among the 33 districts of Assam for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Dhubri (19,49,258) and the least populous is Majuli (1,67,304).

District boundaries change. If Chirang has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Chirang in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Chirang ranks 583rd by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Chirang has about 35% of that. Within Assam, it holds about 2% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Chirang (1,170 sq km) ranks 593rd of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 25th of 27 in Assam. The largest in Assam is Kamrup (6,882 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 412 people for every square kilometre. That is close to the figure for Assam as a whole, about 370, worked out from the same district figures. So it is fairly typical of the state in how closely people live.
