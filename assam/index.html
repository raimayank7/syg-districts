Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Assam?

Assam has 35 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Assam by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Dhubri is the most populous district of Assam, with 19,49,258 people.

Which is the smallest district of Assam by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Majuli is the least populous, with 1,67,304 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Assam by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Kamrup is the largest at 6,882 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
