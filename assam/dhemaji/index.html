Dhemaji district, Assam

Dhemaji is one of the 35 districts of Assam. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 6.9 lakh people.

Population in context

With 6,86,133 people in 2011, Dhemaji ranks 23rd among the 33 districts of Assam for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Dhubri (19,49,258) and the least populous is Majuli (1,67,304).

District boundaries change. If Dhemaji has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Dhemaji in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Dhemaji ranks 528th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Dhemaji has about 50% of that. Within Assam, it holds about 2% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Dhemaji (3,237 sq km) ranks 364th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 9th of 27 in Assam. The largest in Assam is Kamrup (6,882 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 212 people for every square kilometre. That is below the figure for Assam as a whole, about 370, worked out from the same district figures.
