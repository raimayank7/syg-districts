Tamenglong district, Manipur

Tamenglong is one of the 16 districts of Manipur. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 1.4 lakh people.

Population in context

With 1,40,651 people in 2011, Tamenglong ranks 10th among the 16 districts of Manipur for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Imphal West (5,17,992) and the least populous is Jiribam (43,838).

District boundaries change. If Tamenglong has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Tamenglong in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Tamenglong ranks 659th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Tamenglong has about 10% of that. Within Manipur, it holds about 4% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Tamenglong (4,391 sq km) ranks 258th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 3rd of 10 in Manipur. The largest in Manipur is Churachandpur (4,570 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 32 people for every square kilometre. That is well below the figure for Manipur as a whole, about 120, worked out from the same district figures. A low figure usually points to hills, forest, desert or other thinly settled country.
