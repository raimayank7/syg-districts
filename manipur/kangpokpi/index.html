Kangpokpi district, Manipur

Kangpokpi is one of the 16 districts of Manipur. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 1.9 lakh people.

Population in context

With 1,93,744 people in 2011, Kangpokpi ranks 7th among the 16 districts of Manipur for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Imphal West (5,17,992) and the least populous is Jiribam (43,838).

District boundaries change. If Kangpokpi has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Kangpokpi in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Kangpokpi ranks 643rd by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Kangpokpi has about 14% of that. Within Manipur, it holds about 6% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.
