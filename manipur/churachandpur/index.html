Churachandpur district, Manipur

Churachandpur is one of the 16 districts of Manipur. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 2.7 lakh people.

Population in context

With 2,74,143 people in 2011, Churachandpur ranks 5th among the 16 districts of Manipur for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Imphal West (5,17,992) and the least populous is Jiribam (43,838).

District boundaries change. If Churachandpur has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Churachandpur in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Churachandpur ranks 622nd by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Churachandpur has about 20% of that. Within Manipur, it holds about 8% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Churachandpur (4,570 sq km) ranks 231st of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 1st of 10 in Manipur. It is the largest district of Manipur by area.

In 2011 it had about 60 people for every square kilometre. That is below the figure for Manipur as a whole, about 120, worked out from the same district figures.
