North Tripura district, Tripura

North Tripura is one of the 8 districts of Tripura. Its headquarters is Kailashahar. At the 2011 census it had about 6.9 lakh people.

Population in context

With 6,93,947 people in 2011, North Tripura ranks 3rd among the 8 districts of Tripura for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is West Tripura (17,25,739) and the least populous is Unakoti (2,98,574).

District boundaries change. If North Tripura has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
