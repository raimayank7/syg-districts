Districts of Tripura

Tripura is a state with 8 districts in the official directory. Going by 2011 census figures, the most populous is West Tripura (17,25,739 people) and the least populous is Unakoti (2,98,574).

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Tripura?

Tripura has 8 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Tripura by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, West Tripura is the most populous district of Tripura, with 17,25,739 people.

Which is the smallest district of Tripura by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Unakoti is the least populous, with 2,98,574 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Tripura by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, West Tripura is the largest at 2,997 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.

About these facts. The list of districts is the official one from iGOD, the Government of India web directory, as on 11 November 2026. Headquarters, 2011 population, area and other details are from Wikidata, an open database edited by volunteers, read on 11 November 2026. They can contain mistakes.
