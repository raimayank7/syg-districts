Gomati district, Tripura

Gomati is one of the 8 districts of Tripura. Its headquarters is Udaipur. At the 2011 census it had about 4.4 lakh people.

Population in context

With 4,41,538 people in 2011, Gomati ranks 5th among the 8 districts of Tripura for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is West Tripura (17,25,739) and the least populous is Unakoti (2,98,574).

Gomati was formed in 2012, after the 2011 census. The 2011 figure is the one Wikidata records for the area of the district.
