Darbhanga district, Bihar

Darbhanga is one of the 38 districts of Bihar. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 39.4 lakh people.

Population in context

With 39,37,385 people in 2011, Darbhanga ranks 8th among the 38 districts of Bihar for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Patna (58,38,465) and the least populous is Sheikhpura (6,36,342).

District boundaries change. If Darbhanga has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Darbhanga in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Darbhanga ranks 61st by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Darbhanga has about 2.9 times that. Within Bihar, it holds about 4% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Darbhanga (2,279 sq km) ranks 484th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 23rd of 36 in Bihar. The largest in Bihar is Kaimur (Bhabua) (8,268 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 1,728 people for every square kilometre. That is above the figure for Bihar as a whole, about 1,046, worked out from the same district figures.
