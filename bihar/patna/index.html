Patna district, Bihar

Patna is one of the 38 districts of Bihar. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 58.4 lakh people.

Population in context

With 58,38,465 people in 2011, Patna ranks 1st among the 38 districts of Bihar for which we have a 2011 figure. That makes it the most populous of them. The least populous is Sheikhpura (6,36,342).

District boundaries change. If Patna has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
