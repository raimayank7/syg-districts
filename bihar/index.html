Bihar at a glance

The 38 districts with a 2011 figure had about 10.41 crore people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Bhojpur (27,28,407). The most populous, Patna, has about 9.2 times as many as the least populous, Sheikhpura.

By area the largest is Kaimur (Bhabua) (8,268 sq km) and the smallest is Sheohar (444 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Bihar?

Bihar has 38 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Bihar by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Patna is the most populous district of Bihar, with 58,38,465 people.

Which is the smallest district of Bihar by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Sheikhpura is the least populous, with 6,36,342 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.
