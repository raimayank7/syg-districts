Jhajjar district, Haryana

Jhajjar is one of the 23 districts of Haryana. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 9.6 lakh people.

Population in context

With 9,58,405 people in 2011, Jhajjar ranks 17th among the 23 districts of Haryana for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Faridabad (18,09,733) and the least populous is Charkhi Dadri (5,02,276).

District boundaries change. If Jhajjar has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Jhajjar in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Jhajjar ranks 470th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Jhajjar has about 69% of that. Within Haryana, it holds about 4% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Jhajjar (1,890 sq km) ranks 524th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 10th of 22 in Haryana. The largest in Haryana is Bhiwani (5,140 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 507 people for every square kilometre. That is close to the figure for Haryana as a whole, about 548, worked out from the same district figures. So it is fairly typical of the state in how closely people live.
