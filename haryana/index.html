Haryana at a glance

The 23 districts with a 2011 figure had about 2.64 crore people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Nuh (10,89,263). The most populous, Faridabad, has about 3.6 times as many as the least populous, Charkhi Dadri.

By area the largest is Bhiwani (5,140 sq km) and the smallest is Panchkula (816 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Haryana?

Haryana has 23 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Haryana by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Faridabad is the most populous district of Haryana, with 18,09,733 people.

Which is the smallest district of Haryana by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Charkhi Dadri is the least populous, with 5,02,276 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Haryana by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Bhiwani is the largest at 5,140 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.
