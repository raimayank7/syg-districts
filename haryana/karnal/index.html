Karnal district, Haryana

Karnal is one of the 23 districts of Haryana. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 15.1 lakh people.

Population in context

With 15,05,324 people in 2011, Karnal ranks 5th among the 23 districts of Haryana for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Faridabad (18,09,733) and the least populous is Charkhi Dadri (5,02,276).

District boundaries change. If Karnal has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
