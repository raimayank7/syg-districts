Bemetara district, Chhattisgarh

Bemetara is one of the 33 districts of Chhattisgarh. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 8 lakh people.

Population in context

With 7,95,759 people in 2011, Bemetara ranks 15th among the 30 districts of Chhattisgarh for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Raipur (40,63,872) and the least populous is Narayanpur (1,39,820).

District boundaries change. If Bemetara has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Bemetara in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Bemetara ranks 503rd by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Bemetara has about 58% of that. Within Chhattisgarh, it holds about 2% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Bemetara (2,855 sq km) ranks 414th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 22nd of 22 in Chhattisgarh. It is the smallest district of Chhattisgarh by area.

In 2011 it had about 279 people for every square kilometre. That is above the figure for Chhattisgarh as a whole, about 180, worked out from the same district figures.
