Pauri Garhwal district, Uttarakhand

Pauri Garhwal is one of the 13 districts of Uttarakhand. Its headquarters is Pauri. At the 2011 census it had about 6.9 lakh people.

Population in context

With 6,87,271 people in 2011, Pauri Garhwal ranks 5th among the 13 districts of Uttarakhand for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Haridwar (18,90,422) and the least populous is Rudraprayag (2,42,285).

District boundaries change. If Pauri Garhwal has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Pauri Garhwal in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Pauri Garhwal ranks 527th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Pauri Garhwal has about 50% of that. Within Uttarakhand, it holds about 7% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Pauri Garhwal (5,399 sq km) ranks 181st of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 4th of 13 in Uttarakhand. The largest in Uttarakhand is Uttarkashi (8,016 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 127 people for every square kilometre. That is below the figure for Uttarakhand as a whole, about 187, worked out from the same district figures.
