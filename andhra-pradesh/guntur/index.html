Guntur district, Andhra Pradesh

Guntur is one of the 28 districts of Andhra Pradesh. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 48.9 lakh people.

Population in context

With 48,87,813 people in 2011, Guntur ranks 2nd among the 13 districts of Andhra Pradesh for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is East Godavari (51,54,296) and the least populous is Vizianagaram (23,44,474).

District boundaries change. If Guntur has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
