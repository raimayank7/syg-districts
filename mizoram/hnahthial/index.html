Hnahthial district, Mizoram

Hnahthial is one of the 11 districts of Mizoram. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 34,500 people.

Population in context

With 34,524 people in 2011, Hnahthial ranks 11th among the 11 districts of Mizoram for which we have a 2011 figure. That makes it the least populous of them. The most populous is Aizawl (4,00,309).

District boundaries change. If Hnahthial has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
