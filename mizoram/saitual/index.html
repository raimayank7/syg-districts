Saitual district, Mizoram

Saitual is one of the 11 districts of Mizoram. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 50,600 people.

Population in context

With 50,575 people in 2011, Saitual ranks 9th among the 11 districts of Mizoram for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Aizawl (4,00,309) and the least populous is Hnahthial (34,524).

District boundaries change. If Saitual has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Saitual in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Saitual ranks 694th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Saitual has about 4% of that. Within Mizoram, it holds about 4% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.
