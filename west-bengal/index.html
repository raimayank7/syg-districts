Districts of West Bengal

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in West Bengal?

West Bengal has 23 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of West Bengal by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, North 24 Parganas is the most populous district of West Bengal, with 1,00,09,781 people.

Which is the smallest district of West Bengal by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Kalimpong is the least populous, with 2,51,642 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of West Bengal by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, South 24 Parganas is the largest at 9,960 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.

About these facts. The list of districts is the official one from iGOD, the Government of India web directory, as on 11 November 2026. Headquarters, 2011 population, area and other details are from Wikidata, an open database edited by volunteers, read on 11 November 2026. They can contain mistakes. For anything important, check the district's official website. This is not a government site.
