Paschim Bardhaman district, West Bengal

Paschim Bardhaman is one of the 23 districts of West Bengal. Its headquarters is Asansol. At the 2011 census it had about 28.8 lakh people.

Population in context

With 28,82,031 people in 2011, Paschim Bardhaman ranks 17th among the 22 districts of West Bengal for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is North 24 Parganas (1,00,09,781) and the least populous is Kalimpong (2,51,642).

District boundaries change. If Paschim Bardhaman has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
