Purulia district, West Bengal

Purulia is one of the 23 districts of West Bengal. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 29.3 lakh people.

Population in context

With 29,30,115 people in 2011, Purulia ranks 16th among the 22 districts of West Bengal for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is North 24 Parganas (1,00,09,781) and the least populous is Kalimpong (2,51,642).

District boundaries change. If Purulia has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Purulia in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Purulia ranks 129th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Purulia has about 2.1 times that. Within West Bengal, it holds about 3% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Purulia (6,259 sq km) ranks 145th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 4th of 23 in West Bengal. The largest in West Bengal is South 24 Parganas (9,960 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 468 people for every square kilometre. That is well below the figure for West Bengal as a whole, about 1,069, worked out from the same district figures. A low figure usually points to hills, forest, desert or other thinly settled country.
