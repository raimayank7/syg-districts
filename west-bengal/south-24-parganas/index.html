South 24 Parganas district, West Bengal

South 24 Parganas is one of the 23 districts of West Bengal. Its headquarters is Alipore. At the 2011 census it had about 81.6 lakh people.

Population in context

With 81,61,961 people in 2011, South 24 Parganas ranks 2nd among the 22 districts of West Bengal for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is North 24 Parganas (1,00,09,781) and the least populous is Kalimpong (2,51,642).

District boundaries change. If South 24 Parganas has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

South 24 Parganas in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, South 24 Parganas ranks 6th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so South 24 Parganas has about 5.9 times that. Within West Bengal, it holds about 9% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, South 24 Parganas (9,960 sq km) ranks 55th of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 1st of 23 in West Bengal. It is the largest district of West Bengal by area.

In 2011 it had about 819 people for every square kilometre. That is below the figure for West Bengal as a whole, about 1,069, worked out from the same district figures.
