Ramban district, Jammu and Kashmir

Ramban is one of the 20 districts of Jammu and Kashmir, a union territory. Its headquarters is the town of the same name. At the 2011 census it had about 2.8 lakh people.

Population in context

With 2,83,713 people in 2011, Ramban ranks 18th among the 20 districts of Jammu and Kashmir for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Jammu (15,29,958) and the least populous is Kishtwar (2,30,696).

District boundaries change. If Ramban has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.

Ramban in the whole country

Among the 711 districts of India with a 2011 figure on record, Ramban ranks 620th by population. The district in the middle of that list has about 13.8 lakh people, so Ramban has about 21% of that. Within Jammu and Kashmir, it holds about 2% of the people counted in the districts we have figures for.

By area, Ramban (1,329 sq km) ranks 582nd of the 636 districts with an area on record, and 16th of 20 in Jammu and Kashmir. The largest in Jammu and Kashmir is Kishtwar (7,737 sq km).

In 2011 it had about 213 people for every square kilometre. That is below the figure for Jammu and Kashmir as a whole, about 261, worked out from the same district figures.
