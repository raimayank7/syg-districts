Districts of Jammu and Kashmir

Jammu and Kashmir is a union territory with 20 districts in the official directory. Going by 2011 census figures, the most populous is Jammu (15,29,958 people) and the least populous is Kishtwar (2,30,696).

Jammu and Kashmir at a glance

The 20 districts with a 2011 figure had about 1.23 crore people between them. The district in the middle of the list by population is Poonch (4,76,835). The most populous, Jammu, has about 6.6 times as many as the least populous, Kishtwar.

By area the largest is Kishtwar (7,737 sq km) and the smallest is Bandipora (398 sq km). Use the buttons above the table to sort by people or by area, and tap a name for that district's page with its rank, nearby districts and official website.

Reading the table

The names are those in the official directory. "Not recorded" means Wikidata has no value for that district, or the value failed our checks. Population is from the 2011 census, the last full census, so districts created later may show the figure of the area they were carved from, or none.

Questions people ask

How many districts are there in Jammu and Kashmir?

Jammu and Kashmir has 20 districts in the Government of India web directory (iGOD), as on 11 November 2026.

Which is the largest district of Jammu and Kashmir by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Jammu is the most populous district of Jammu and Kashmir, with 15,29,958 people.

Which is the smallest district of Jammu and Kashmir by population?

By 2011 census figures recorded on Wikidata, Kishtwar is the least populous, with 2,30,696 people. Districts without a 2011 figure are not counted.

Which is the largest district of Jammu and Kashmir by area?

Among the districts with an area on record, Kishtwar is the largest at 7,737 sq km, as recorded on Wikidata.

About these facts. The list of districts is the official one from iGOD, the Government of India web directory, as on 11 November 2026.
