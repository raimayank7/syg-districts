Belagavi district, Karnataka

Belagavi is one of the 31 districts of Karnataka. Its headquarters is Belgaum. At the 2011 census it had about 47.8 lakh people.

Population in context

With 47,79,661 people in 2011, Belagavi ranks 2nd among the 30 districts of Karnataka for which we have a 2011 figure. The most populous is Bengaluru Urban (96,21,551) and the least populous is Kodagu (5,54,519).

District boundaries change. If Belagavi has been divided or redrawn since 2011, the figure is for the district as it was then.
